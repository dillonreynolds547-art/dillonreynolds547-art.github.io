Dynasties Untold Uploader

A personal publishing tool for the Dynasties Untold YouTube channel.

Dynasties Untold is a YouTube channel of short documentary videos about the history of ancient China. This uploader is the private tool the channel's owner uses to publish those videos. It runs on the owner's own computer and uploads finished videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.

The tool is not offered to the public. It only ever signs in to the channel owner's Google account, and it does not read, collect or store anyone else's data.

What it uses YouTube access for

  • Uploading the channel's own videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • Reading the channel's name, to confirm it is signed in to the right channel.
